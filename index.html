FROM THE SWEAT LAB

The Gatorade Sports Science Institute (GSSI) is a global team of PhDs, dietitians and physiologists measuring sweat on courts, tracks and training pitches in 38 countries.

Meet the researchers
FIELD STUDIES

4,217 athletes
measured since 2008.

Patch-tested. Weighed in. Weighed out. Every dataset a new recipe. Our average study produces 11,800 data points across nine biometric channels.

SODIUM LOSS

1.13–2.34 g / hr

Range observed across football, tennis and endurance running in heat. Gatorade Endurance doubles standard sodium to match heavy sweat rates.

CARB ABSORPTION

Multiple Transportable
Glucose + Fructose.

A 4:1 glucose-to-fructose ratio uses both SGLT1 and GLUT5 pathways, enabling up to 90 g of carbs per hour without GI distress.

CASE FILE

MARATHON DES SABLES, 2024

Six stages, 251 km, 47°C. Twenty-eight runners fueled on Gatorade Endurance finished 6.8% faster than the carbohydrate control group. Notes from the field log are appended to the published dataset.
